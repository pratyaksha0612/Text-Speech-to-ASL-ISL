Understanding Indian Sign Language (ISL)

Origin & Standardization

ISL is a natural visual language co-developed by deaf schools and associations across India. Standardized by the Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre (ISLRTC), it serves as the primary visual language of the Indian deaf community, featuring its own complete grammar rules.

Linguistic Differences

Unlike ASL which is a one-handed system, ISL primarily utilizes a two-handed fingerspelling system for letters, along with distinct hand configurations, facial expressions, and spatial positioning. Standardized dictionaries by ISLRTC now catalog thousands of visual signs.
