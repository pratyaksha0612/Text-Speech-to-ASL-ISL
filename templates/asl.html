Understanding American Sign Language (ASL)

Origin & Founders

ASL was co-founded in 1817 by Thomas Hopkins Gallaudet and French educator Laurent Clerc. They established the American School for the Deaf in Hartford, Connecticut. Since then, it has grown as the primary visual language of the North American Deaf community.

Evolution & Tech Changes

ASL split from French Sign Language (LSF) to establish its own unique, independent grammar structure, entirely distinct from English. Over time, ASL has incorporated contemporary vocabulary for internet age terminology, smart devices, and social media, ensuring it remains fluid and modern.

Interactive A-Z Reference

The A-Z reference tool plays corresponding sign videos when clicked. ASL uses a one-handed finger-spelling system for letters A through Z. This system is crucial for spelling names, proper nouns, and words for which there are no standalone signs.
