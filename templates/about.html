About Vyakt

Communication should never depend on ability.

Where It Began

Vyakt originated as a technical exploration into sign language recognition. The initial goal was to understand whether machines could interpret human gestures and translate them into structured language with meaningful accuracy.

The early phase focused heavily on computer vision, gesture detection, and mapping movements to alphabets and words using trained models.

What started as a learning exercise quickly evolved into something far more significant.

The Realization

During development, an important realization emerged, sign language is not a simplified version of spoken language. It is a complete linguistic system with its own grammar, expression, and cultural depth.

Most existing systems treat sign language as secondary, often forcing users to adapt to text or speech-based interfaces.

This revealed a deeper issue: accessibility is often treated as an afterthought, rather than a core design principle.

The Problem

Communication technologies today are largely built around sound and text. For millions of individuals who rely on sign language, this creates a constant barrier in everyday interactions.

Whether in classrooms, workplaces, or digital platforms, the absence of inclusive communication tools limits participation and accessibility.

This gap highlighted the need for systems that support visual communication natively.

The Approach

Vyakt was designed with a different philosophy, technology should adapt to people, not the other way around.

The system integrates machine learning models, gesture recognition, and real-time processing to convert speech and text into sign language.

The focus remains on building a system that is efficient, lightweight, and usable in real-world scenarios without heavy computational requirements.

The Build

The development process involved dataset preparation, model training, optimization, and integration into a web-based interface.

The system supports both text and speech input, translating them into visual sign language output using pre-trained models and structured mappings.

Special care was taken to ensure smooth performance on low-resource systems, making the solution practical and accessible.

The Impact

Vyakt aims to reduce communication barriers by enabling real-time translation between spoken language and sign language.

It creates opportunities for inclusive interaction in education, workplaces, and digital communication platforms.

More importantly, it shifts the perspective from accommodation to empowerment.

The Vision

Vyakt is not just a tool, it is a step toward building communication systems that are inherently inclusive.

The long-term vision includes real-time translation, multilingual sign support, and integration across platforms to enable seamless communication.

The goal is simple: a world where communication is not limited by ability.

Vyakt is not just translation.
It is accessibility.
