How VYAKT Works

VYAKT transforms language into visual communication - preserving meaning, emotion, and accessibility in real time.

Speech → Sign

VYAKT listens to spoken language, understands it, and instantly renders it into sign language visuals. This allows conversations to happen naturally, without requiring reading or interpretation.

Text → Sign

Written words are transformed into expressive visual language, making digital communication accessible to those who understand sign language as their primary form of communication.

Real-Time Translation

Built for live interaction - enabling inclusive conversations in classrooms, meetings, and everyday life. VYAKT is designed to evolve into seamless real-time communication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sign language is a natural visual language with its own grammar and structure, distinct from text. VYAKT translates text and speech into sign configurations, allowing users to communicate in their native primary visual form.

VYAKT leverages browser-native speech recognition APIs. Speech is converted to text locally on your device in real time, and the system matches the text to our database of high-quality sign language videos instantly.

ASL (American Sign Language) is primarily used in North America and employs a one-handed manual alphabet. ISL (Indian Sign Language) is standardized by the ISLRTC in India and utilizes distinctive two-handed configurations and gestures.

No. By offloading processing dynamically, VYAKT runs real-time sign detection on standard CPUs in web browsers using optimized MediaPipe landmark keypoint coordinates combined with a Random Forest classifier.

VYAKT's sign detection portal integrates a Random Forest Classifier trained on preprocessed MediaPipe landmark vectors, utilizing normalized coordinate points and wrist angles for high precision.

The real-time detector is currently optimized to recognize static ASL alphabet gestures letter-by-letter. Word building is supported via hold gestures, and sentence autocomplete engines are planned for subsequent phases.

The translation engine parses input sentences, extracts key sign markers, retrieves corresponding sign video segments, and stitches them together for a seamless sequential sign playback with adjustable speed.

The long-term vision of VYAKT is to expand into a universal communication extension for video conferencing platforms, allowing real-time, zero-latency sign-to-speech and speech-to-sign translations globally.
