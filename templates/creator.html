The Journey of Vyakt

Vyakt started with a simple thought: communication should never feel like a barrier. That idea slowly turned into a project where I explored AI, web development, accessibility, and design, all together.

From training models and handling the backend to designing the interface and deploying the application, every part of Vyakt has been built, broken, rebuilt, and improved by me. It has taught me far more than any classroom ever could.

Learning by Building
