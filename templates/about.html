About VYAKT

Communication should never depend on ability.

The Spark

The journey began as a college exploration into gesture recognition. The goal was to build a system that could identify fingerspelling configurations and translate them into text.

Experimenting with real-time hand-shape tracking showed that technology could directly empower visual expression.

This work sparked a deep connection to inclusive design and assistive communication.

The Realization

An important realization emerged: sign language is not a secondary representation of spoken language. It is a complete, natural linguistic system with its own deep grammar and expression.

Differently abled individuals are not "different"—their primary visual language is their normal, and they deserve to be treated with complete equality.

This insight shifted the perspective from visual accommodation to true communication parity.

The Evolution

We set out to build VYAKT to move beyond simple letter recognition to a complete translation ecosystem.

We designed VYAKT to translate text and voice into sequential sign language animations, giving speech a natural visual presence.

The tool provides translation, custom speed pacing, and interactive reference guides to serve real communication needs.

The Vision

Our end goal is to make visual communication as simple, seamless, and normal for signers as spoken communication is for everyone else.

We are working toward integrating real-time sign detection, expanding vocabulary datasets, and exploring integration as browser or meeting extensions.

VYAKT is built on the belief that communication is a fundamental right.

VYAKT is not just translation.
It is accessibility.
