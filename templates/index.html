How VYAKT Works

VYAKT transforms language into visual communication - preserving meaning, emotion, and accessibility in real time.

Speech → Sign Engine

VYAKT listens to spoken language using browser-native speech recognition and instantly renders it into sign language video sequences. This allows natural conversation without reading barriers.

Text → Sign Engine

Written text is parsed and converted into sequential visual sign language animations, empowering signers to communicate in their native visual medium.

Interactive Learn Hub

Master ASL & ISL step-by-step with dedicated A–Z letter modules, high-definition sign demonstrations, gesture formation guides, and custom playback speed controls.

Sign Practice Quiz

Test your visual sign recognition with jumbled ASL & ISL questions. Wrong answers reveal step-by-step hand guides with 'Try Again' and 'Pass' options.

Expandable Video Gallery

Explore sign reference videos in high resolution with left/right step navigation arrows and a 26-letter interactive thumbnail carousel bar.

Ashi AI Companion

Get instant guidance from Ashi, our emotionally intelligent multi-lingual AI assistant, supporting 6 global languages for total platform accessibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about VYAKT, its purpose, and its future.

Sign language is a natural visual language with its own grammar and structure, distinct from text. VYAKT translates text and speech into sign configurations, allowing users to communicate in their native primary visual form.

VYAKT leverages browser-native speech recognition APIs. Speech is converted to text locally on your device in real time, and the system matches the text to our database of high-quality sign language videos instantly.

Yes. Every translator page features a playback speed slider allowing you to adjust the frame pace from 0.25x (slower pacing, ideal for learning) up to 2.00x (faster pacing for native signers).

ASL (American Sign Language) is primarily used in North America and employs a one-handed manual alphabet. ISL (Indian Sign Language) is standardized by the ISLRTC in India and utilizes distinctive two-handed configurations and gestures.

Ashi is VYAKT's built-in conversational companion. She is designed to guide you through the platform, resolve your queries about the system's usage, and answer questions about sign language accessibility in multiple languages.

No. By offloading processing dynamically, VYAKT runs real-time sign detection on standard CPUs in web browsers using optimized MediaPipe landmark keypoint coordinates combined with a Random Forest classifier.

VYAKT's sign detection portal integrates a Random Forest Classifier trained on preprocessed MediaPipe landmark vectors, utilizing normalized coordinate points and wrist angles for high precision.

The real-time detector is currently optimized to recognize static ASL alphabet gestures letter-by-letter. Word building is supported via hold gestures, and sentence autocomplete engines are planned for subsequent phases.

The translation engine parses input sentences, extracts key sign markers, retrieves corresponding sign video segments, and stitches them together for a seamless sequential sign playback with adjustable speed.

The Learn Hub (/learn) is a dedicated study module for both ASL and ISL. You can select any letter from A-Z or navigate step-by-step. Each letter displays high-definition video demonstrations alongside step-by-step hand gesture formation guides.

The Sign Practice Quiz randomly jumbles ASL and ISL questions to test your sign recognition. If you make a mistake, an interactive teaching card opens detailing how to sign that specific letter, giving you options to 'Try Again' until correct or 'Pass' to the next question.

Clicking any reference card on the ASL or ISL A-Z pages opens an expandable gallery modal. It features an enlarged video player, left and right step arrows, and a 26-letter interactive thumbnail carousel bar for fast navigation.

Ashi is an emotionally intelligent AI assistant capable of multi-lingual onboarding (English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, and Italian). She validates user profiles, guides you through platform features, and answers questions about sign language accessibility.

The long-term vision of VYAKT is to expand into a universal communication extension for video conferencing platforms, allowing real-time, zero-latency sign-to-speech and speech-to-sign translations globally.
