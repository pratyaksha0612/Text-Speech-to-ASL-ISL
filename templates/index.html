How Vyakt Works

Vyakt transforms language into visual communication — preserving meaning, emotion, and accessibility in real time.

Speech → Sign

Vyakt listens to spoken language, understands it, and instantly renders it into sign language visuals. This allows conversations to happen naturally, without requiring reading or interpretation.

Text → Sign

Written words are transformed into expressive visual language, making digital communication accessible to those who understand sign language as their primary form of communication.

Real-Time Translation

Built for live interaction — enabling inclusive conversations in classrooms, meetings, and everyday life. Vyakt is designed to evolve into seamless real-time communication.
