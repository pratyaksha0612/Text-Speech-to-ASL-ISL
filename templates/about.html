VYAKT

Communication should never depend on ability.

Where It Began

Vyakt began as an exploration into sign language recognition. The initial question was technical: could machines understand human gestures and translate them into language?

But while building the system a deeper realization emerged. Sign language is not a substitute for spoken language. It is a complete language with its own grammar, expression and cultural depth.

The Realization

Most technology expects people to adapt to it. Communication tools assume that language must exist as either sound or text.

For millions of people this assumption does not hold. For them communication is visual. That realization shaped the idea behind Vyakt.

The Idea

Instead of forcing sign language users to rely on written language, technology should adapt to the language that feels natural to them.

Vyakt transforms spoken and written communication into visual sign language — making conversations accessible in classrooms, workplaces and everyday digital spaces.

The Future

Vyakt is designed to evolve into a real-time bridge between spoken language and sign language.

A system where conversations flow naturally between people regardless of how they communicate.

Vyakt is not just translation.
It is accessibility.
