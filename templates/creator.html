PRATYAKSHA SINGH

VYAKT represents a deeply personal journey of building technology with human purpose. It all started when I built a simple hand-shape detection system as my early college exploration, recognizing static sign configurations using computer vision.

Developing that project opened my eyes to how visual sign systems work and ignited a passion for accessibility. I realized that fingerspelling was only a small window into a vast world. I wanted to build a complete, real visual communication ecosystem. This inspiration led directly to the creation of VYAKT.

My ultimate goal is to make visual communication natural, equal, and simple for the differently abled. They are not "different"—sign language is their normal, and we must treat them with complete equality. I am committed to evolving VYAKT into a fully universal platform, bridging communication gaps seamlessly for everyone.
